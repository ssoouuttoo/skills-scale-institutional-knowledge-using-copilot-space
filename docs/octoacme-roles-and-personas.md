# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business or Product Owner

### Role Summary
The Business or Product Owner defines the desired outcomes, prioritizes the highest-value work, and represents customer or stakeholder needs throughout the project lifecycle. This role ensures the project stays aligned to business value and decision-making priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define the business problem, desired outcomes, and success metrics
- Prioritize scope, backlog, and trade-offs across competing demands
- Provide product or service context to the project team and stakeholders
- Review proposed solutions and validate whether they satisfy business needs
- Resolve conflicts between customer needs, delivery constraints, and strategic priorities

### Goals
- Maximize value delivered to customers and stakeholders
- Keep scope, priorities, and trade-offs transparent and aligned to strategy
- Support faster and more confident delivery decisions

### Typical Communication
- Product strategy reviews and stakeholder briefings
- Prioritization and roadmap discussions with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Acceptance reviews and business readiness checks at milestones

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager on schedule, scope trade-offs, and stakeholder communication
- Provides direction to Developers and Product Managers on business intent and priority order
- Collaborates with the Sponsor for funding, governance, and strategic alignment
- Shares decisions and outcomes with the Change and Adoption Lead to support readiness and rollout

---

## Technical Lead or Solution Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Solution Architect provides technical direction, drives architectural decisions, and helps teams design solutions that meet both business goals and technical quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, patterns, and standards for the solution
- Evaluate design options, trade-offs, and technical risks
- Guide implementation decisions with Developers and cross-functional partners
- Ensure alignment between the solution design and project objectives
- Support dependency planning, integration strategy, and delivery sequencing

### Goals
- Deliver a solution that is scalable, maintainable, and appropriate for the business context
- Reduce design ambiguity and rework early in the lifecycle
- Improve technical confidence across the project team

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Dependency and integration planning with engineering and delivery leads
- Escalation support when technical decisions affect scope, schedule, or risk

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers and the Business Owner to translate requirements into feasible technical decisions
- Works closely with Project Managers to understand delivery dependencies and sequencing
- Provides technical guidance and risk insights to Developers and Delivery Team leads
- Coordinates with the Quality and Compliance Lead on design review, validation, and control requirements

---

## Project Sponsor or Steering Committee Representative

### Role Summary
The Project Sponsor or Steering Committee Representative provides executive sponsorship, strategic direction, and governance support so the project can move forward with appropriate resources and stakeholder alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Sponsor the project vision and business case
- Approve major scope, funding, resourcing, and timeline decisions
- Resolve escalations that require executive authority or organizational support
- Help remove barriers that are outside the delivery team's control
- Monitor strategic outcomes and business value realization

### Goals
- Ensure the project remains aligned to organizational priorities
- Support successful decision-making and issue resolution at the executive level
- Increase confidence in the project’s direction and expected outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee meetings and executive updates
- Major milestone reviews and issue escalations
- Business case and investment decision briefings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receives escalations from the Project Manager when major risks, trade-offs, or blockers require sponsor attention
- Aligns with the Business or Product Owner on priorities, value realization, and strategic goals
- Collaborates with Technical Leads on major architecture, investment, or resource trade-offs
- Works with the Change and Adoption Lead to ensure readiness and adoption align with business goals

---

## Delivery Team or Workstream Lead

### Role Summary
The Delivery Team or Workstream Lead coordinates execution within a functional area or workstream, ensuring that planned work is completed, dependencies are managed, and progress is visible to the broader project team.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate team execution for a specific workstream or domain
- Plan, track, and report status against commitments and dependencies
- Identify blockers, capacity constraints, and cross-team dependencies
- Support communication between engineering, product, and project stakeholders
- Ensure quality and completion standards are met before handoff or release

### Goals
- Deliver predictable progress across the workstream
- Reduce ambiguity, delays, and unplanned churn
- Improve team accountability and alignment with project milestones

### Typical Communication
- Workstream planning and daily standups
- Dependency updates and readiness reviews
- Reporting into project status and milestone check-ins

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Reports progress and risks to the Project Manager for portfolio or project-level coordination
- Works with Developers to translate plans into team commitments and execution actions
- Coordinates with the Technical Lead on technical dependencies and delivery sequencing
- Supports the Business or Product Owner by surfacing delivery progress and trade-off impacts

---

## Quality or Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality or Compliance Lead ensures quality expectations, controls, and compliance requirements are defined and met before work is accepted or released.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality criteria, validation criteria, and review expectations
- Coordinate testing, quality assurance, and compliance review activities
- Identify gaps in controls, documentation, and release readiness
- Support risk reduction for quality, security, and operational readiness
- Validate that work meets agreed standards before milestones are closed

### Goals
- Improve confidence in the quality and completeness of deliverables
- Reduce defects, rework, and compliance-related issues
- Ensure readiness for release and stakeholder acceptance

### Typical Communication
- Quality reviews, validation checkpoints, and test planning
- Compliance and readiness reviews with project and delivery stakeholders
- Escalation of quality or governance concerns to the Project Manager or Sponsor

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Reviews technical implementation with the Technical Lead and Developers
- Works with Project Managers to ensure quality gates and readiness criteria are visible in plans
- Provides guidance to the Business or Product Owner on acceptance criteria and release confidence
- Partners with the Change and Adoption Lead to validate that the user experience and operational readiness align with stakeholder expectations

---

## Change and Adoption Lead

### Role Summary
The Change and Adoption Lead helps prepare people and teams for new processes, tools, or workflows so that adoption is sustained after delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify stakeholder groups, communication needs, and change impacts
- Plan communications, training, and readiness activities across the project lifecycle
- Support adoption monitoring and feedback collection after rollout
- Coordinate stakeholder enablement with business and delivery teams
- Help translate project changes into manageable operational steps for end users

### Goals
- Improve adoption and sustained use of new processes or solutions
- Reduce resistance, confusion, and productivity loss during transitions
- Support a smoother path from delivery to operational reality

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder communications and training plans
- Adoption readiness reviews and feedback loops
- Release and rollout readiness briefings with project leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with the Product Manager and Business Owner on stakeholder needs and desired outcomes
- Works with the Project Manager to include adoption activities in the project plan and milestone tracking
- Coordinates with the Technical Lead on rollout practicality and change impacts
- Supports delivery and release quality by ensuring users have the information they need before go-live
